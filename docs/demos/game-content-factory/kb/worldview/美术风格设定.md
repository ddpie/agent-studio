# 美术风格设定：《幻夜之刃》

本文档定义《幻夜之刃》项目的视觉风格。Agent 在调用 `generate_image` 或 `create_storyboard` 时，应先检索本文档，将下方的 style_context 字符串作为 `style_context` 参数传入。

---

## style_context 字符串

> 直接作为 `style_context` 参数传给 generate_image / create_storyboard。

```
Japanese dark fantasy, ink-wash painting meets digital concept art, twilight sky of deep indigo fading to muted purple, star fragments as ambient particles, soft volumetric god-rays piercing through dusk clouds, warm gold and cool silver rim lighting, melancholic yet elegant atmosphere, restrained color palette with desaturated tones punctuated by selective luminous accents, traditional East Asian architectural silhouettes, flowing fabric and organic curves, cinematic composition with atmospheric depth
```

---

## 各维度详细设定

### 1. 画风 / 技法

水墨画（ink-wash painting）的笔触肌理与数字概念艺术的精细度融合。线条保留手绘质感（略带飞白和干笔效果），但色彩层次和光影采用现代 CG 渲染。

- 主导风格：Japanese ink-wash digital painting
- 辅助技法：soft particle effects for star fragments, subtle paper texture overlay
- 英文关键词：`ink-wash painting meets digital concept art, hand-drawn linework with dry-brush texture, soft CG lighting and particle effects`

### 2. 色调 / 配色

夜见是暮色永驻的世界——天空永远处于黎明前/黄昏后的深蓝到暗紫过渡。色调整体压暗、去饱和，仅在关键视觉焦点处使用高亮度的点缀色。

- 主色调：deep indigo (#1a1a3e), twilight purple (#3d2b5a), charcoal blue (#2c3e50)
- 辅助色/点缀色：star gold (#d4a843), moonlight silver (#c0c7d1), cherry blossom pink (#e8a0b4)
- 禁用色：纯白色背景、霓虹色、高饱和暖色大面积使用
- 英文关键词：`twilight palette, desaturated indigo-to-purple gradient sky, selective luminous accents of warm gold and cool silver, muted tones with sparse cherry-blossom pink highlights`

### 3. 光影

主光源模拟永恒黄昏：低角度的余晖从画面边缘渗入，形成体积光（god-rays）。辅助光为星光粒子的冷色散射。阴影不使用纯黑，而是带紫调的暗色。

- 主光源：low-angle twilight god-rays, warm gold tone
- 辅助光：ambient starlight scatter, cool silver-blue
- 阴影：soft gradient shadows with purple undertone, never pure black
- 英文关键词：`soft volumetric god-rays piercing dusk clouds, warm gold and cool silver rim lighting, purple-tinted soft shadows, ambient star-particle glow`

### 4. 构图倾向

电影宽幅构图为主，强调空气纵深感（前景剪影 + 中景主体 + 远景消融于雾气）。人物构图偏中近景，留白用于强化孤独感/诗意。

- 视角：slightly low angle or eye-level, occasionally bird's-eye for landscapes
- 景深：atmospheric perspective with foreground silhouettes and distant haze
- 画幅：cinematic widescreen (16:9) for scenes, portrait (3:4) for characters
- 英文关键词：`cinematic composition with atmospheric depth, foreground silhouettes framing the subject, distant haze and layered depth, generous negative space`

### 5. 氛围关键词

夜见世界的情绪底色是"在漫长暗夜中寻找彼岸光明"——忧郁但不绝望，孤寂但有温度。

- 核心氛围：melancholic, elegant, mysterious
- 叙事基调：restrained, poetic, bittersweet longing
- 环境感：ancient, sacred, twilight-eternal
- 英文关键词：`melancholic yet elegant atmosphere, restrained poetic mood, sense of ancient mystery, bittersweet twilight-eternal world`

### 6. 负面提示 / 全局禁忌

以下内容在任何生成中必须排除（附加到 negative_prompt 参数）：

```
modern clothing, smartphones, cars, neon signs, bright daylight, pure white background, chibi style, anime moe aesthetics, oversaturated colors, text overlays, watermarks, signature, western medieval armor, guns, sci-fi elements, realistic photographic style
```

## 使用指引

1. 生成角色相关图片时，将本文档的 style_context 字符串 + 对应角色视觉锚点拼接后传入。
2. 生成场景/环境图时，仅使用 style_context 字符串 + 具体场景描述。
3. negative_prompt 建议同时传入第 6 节的全局禁忌列表。
4. 如需生成 UI/图标等非世界观内容，不传 style_context 即可恢复默认行为。
